# Contact & Junction Bench

Interactive band diagrams, space charge, electric field, I–V and C–V for metal–semiconductor contacts, semiconductor homo/heterojunctions, MOS capacitors and compound-semiconductor heterostructures — quantum wells, superlattices, HEMTs (AlGaAs/GaAs, pHEMT, AlGaN/GaN), InGaN LEDs, GRIN-SCH lasers, graded HBTs and resonant tunneling diodes — with a self-consistent Schrödinger–Poisson solver (300 K). Single self-contained `index.html`.

Live: https://muonide.github.io/junction-bench/ — append `#ms`, `#hj`, `#mos` or `#hs` to open a specific tab.
