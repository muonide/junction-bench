# Contact & Junction Bench

Interactive band diagrams, space charge, electric field, I–V and C–V for metal–semiconductor contacts, semiconductor homo/heterojunctions, MOS capacitors and compound-semiconductor heterostructures — quantum wells, superlattices, HEMTs (AlGaAs/GaAs, pHEMT, AlGaN/GaN), InGaN LEDs, GRIN-SCH lasers, graded HBTs, resonant tunneling diodes, ITO-anode OLED stacks, and silicon-heterojunction and perovskite solar cells under 1-sun illumination (drift–diffusion) — with a self-consistent Schrödinger–Poisson solver (300 K). Fermi–Dirac statistics with Mott-transition ionization throughout, so degenerate cases (Esaki tunnel diodes, ITO/Si junctions) are handled, with Kane band-to-band tunneling. Single self-contained `index.html`.

Every exemplar has two sets of notes: an introductory explanation in Basic mode (upper-level undergraduate) and a graduate-level discussion in Advanced mode. Each tab's "Model, equations and further reading" section documents the equations the solver uses, with references.


Live: https://muonide.github.io/junction-bench/ — append `#ms`, `#hj`, `#mos` or `#hs` to open a specific tab.
